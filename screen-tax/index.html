What The Screen Tax is.

The Screen Tax is the value of the up-to-8 hours per week each care worker loses to administrative screen work instead of care (BMC Geriatrics, Ausserhofer et al., 2023), priced at the agency’s own rates and headcount.

The label discipline matters: the Screen Tax is the caregiver-hours figure. The Tangleware Tax™ is the larger, whole-agency figure that includes the owner’s and office’s reconciliation work. They are paired to census and never crossed: $24K is the Screen Tax at five caregivers; $144K is the Screen Tax at thirty; $247K is the Tangleware Tax™ at fifteen (CareBravo CareDrain™ Calculator methodology).

Up to 40% of every paid shift goes to administrative tasks (BMC Geriatrics, 2023). The Screen Tax is what that looks like on a payroll.

What owners ask about The Screen Tax.

How much is the Screen Tax at my agency?

Up to 8 hours per week per care worker (BMC Geriatrics, Ausserhofer et al., 2023) times your caregivers times your loaded rate. At five caregivers it comes to about $24,000 a year; at thirty, about $144,000 (CareBravo CareDrain™ Calculator methodology). The Calculator does it with your numbers.

I have eight patients and four caregivers. Is that even worth calculating?

Four caregivers at up to 8 hours a week is up to 32 hours — most of a full-time caregiver you are paying for and not getting. At your size CareBravo lands on the $250 floor; the first thing it returns is those hours.

I have 30 caregivers. Is $144K the Screen Tax or the whole drain?

The Screen Tax only. The whole-agency figure, the Tangleware Tax™, is larger because it includes the office’s reconciliation work. At 30 caregivers you are at the edge of two personas; the Calculator pairs the right figure to your census so the labels are never crossed.

Is the 8 hours a week figure real?

Yes: BMC Geriatrics, Ausserhofer et al., 2023, measured administrative burden on care staff at up to 8 hours per week. It is the one number on this site we repeat most, and always with the source.

How does CareBravo reduce the Screen Tax?

By making the visit verify once and the note draft itself for the nurse to sign, so the caregiver’s screen time is the clock-in and the exception. The hours return to care; the record is complete anyway.
