What is the best software for a Medicaid home care agency?

For a Medicaid HCBS agency the test is not the feature list but who does the work after the software is installed. Most home care software hands the scheduling, billing, EVV and compliance work back to the owner and calls it a service. CareBravo is more than software: it does the nine jobs of a Medicaid agency on one record, on the Autonomous Care OS®, with the owner signing only what an owner must sign. What is an Autonomous Care OS?

An Autonomous Care OS® is an operating system for a care agency in which the administrative work is carried by the system rather than handed back to the people who care. CareBravo’s runs nine agents, one per function, orchestrated by Cary®, on one record, inside the owner’s rules. It is built on the principle of Autonomous Caring®: people who care should care, and up to 40% of every paid shift currently goes to administrative work instead (BMC Geriatrics, 2023).
