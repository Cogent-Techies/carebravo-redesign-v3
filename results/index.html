The same nine lines. At your size most weeks say: covered, out clean, nothing open, one decision for you. The report is how you know the $250 bought work, not a login.

At 90 patients, what numbers can I actually state after 90 days?

First-pass clean rate, days to fill a shift, authorization-to-bill cycle, 100-day retention, referrals by source — weekly, dated, by payer where it applies. Those are the numbers an MCO network, and eventually a buyer, asks for first.

What does “what went wrong on our side” mean?

Exactly that. If a claim was transmitted late, a renewal was requested late, or a callout wasn’t matched in time because of us, it is on the report that week, with the resolution and its date. Every week carries the line, including the weeks it says nothing.
