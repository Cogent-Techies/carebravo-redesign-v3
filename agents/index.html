What owners ask about Cary.

Is Cary a chatbot?

No. Cary® is the one agent you address — by text or voice — and it orchestrates nine agents, one per job, that do the work on your record. A chatbot answers; Cary reports what was done and asks you only the question an owner has to answer.

I have eight patients. Do I need nine agents?

You need the evenings back, and the agents are how that happens at any size. Under about 17 active clients Autonomous lands on the $250 floor, the same as Core; the difference is whether the callout, the claim check and the exception are yours to do or done inside your rules.

My office manager runs the day. What does Cary do for her at 90 patients?

It takes the routine off her and leaves the judgment: every callout matched, claim checked, exception worked and renewal requested inside the rules you set, reported weekly and dated. She spends her day on the calls that need a person.

Who signs the clinical documentation?

The nurse, every time. The record drafts; the nurse reviews and signs every clinical field. A note the nurse has not signed is not a note.

Is this software, or agents doing work?

More than software. The platform is where the work lives on every plan; Cary and the nine agents are how it gets done on Autonomous, inside your rules, with you signing what only an owner signs.
