# CareBravo — Image Shot List v1 (17 September 2026)

One entry per image slot on the site. The HTML already references every filename below; generate the file, drop it at the path, nothing else changes.

## The canon (applies to every prompt)
- Source of truth: file 17 v2.1 visual bible. Every still is generated and art-directed; no stock; no real customer, caregiver or client; no recognisable faces (turned away, cropped, or out of focus); no screens, UI, clipboards or medical equipment; no text or logos baked in.
- Palette: warm paper, stone, ink; natural light; one small copper object in frame. Film grain. Documentary realism.
- Heroes carry a white headline in the lower-left: keep that region quiet and dark enough (the page also applies a bottom gradient).
- Deliver JPEG, quality 82, sRGB, at the pixel size given. Heroes additionally get a 4:3 mobile crop only if the 16:9/21:9 composition loses the subject on a phone; otherwise the page crops with `object-fit: cover`.
- OG cards: 1200×630, no text baked in.

Total: 70 files (25 OG cards, 45 page stills).
