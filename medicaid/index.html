What Medicaid owners ask.

Is the 80/20 rule real, and when does it take effect?

It is real and it is one dated episode: the CMS Medicaid Access Rule of May 2024 set an 80% direct-care-worker pass-through effective 2030, and it is under active OMB rescission review. Plan for the envelope, not the episode: rates compress, state clocks run, and the workforce math doesn’t wait for 2030.

I have eight waiver patients. Is CareBravo built for an agency my size?

It was built in Georgia for exactly that agency. Under about 17 active clients Core and Autonomous both land on the $250 floor; what you get first is the first claim out clean and the authorization chased before it stalls.

I run 90 patients across fee-for-service and two plans. What changes?

Every payer’s rules on one record — units, authorizations, EVV, documentation — and a weekly, dated view by payer: first-pass rate, denials with causes, AR aging. What you gain is the set of numbers a plan network asks for when it decides which agencies it keeps.

Does CareBravo bill Medicaid managed care as well as fee-for-service?

Yes. Waiver programs, managed care organizations, Medicare-certified home health where applicable, VA, long-term care insurance and private pay from the same record. Payer-agnostic in scope; Medicaid-first in design.

Is this Medicaid billing software or something else?

More than software. The platform is waiver-native on every plan; the plan decides who carries the work — your team, the nine agents, or our billing specialists behind the written 99% guarantee.
