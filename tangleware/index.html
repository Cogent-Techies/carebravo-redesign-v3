What Tangleware™ is.

Tangleware™ is the tangle of disconnected systems a home care agency operates — scheduling in one, EVV in another, billing in a third, documentation in a fourth — where the work of making them agree falls on the owner and her staff, every week.

Each tool does its job. None does the agency’s job. The visit is verified in one system, the claim is built in another, the note lives in a third, and on Friday night someone reconciles them by hand. The Tangleware Tax™ is what that costs: at a 15-caregiver agency, about $247,000 a year (CareBravo CareDrain™ Calculator methodology).

Yes, in hours rather than dollars: the Friday reconciliation, the claim rebuilt from the schedule, the note retyped into the billing system. At your size CareBravo lands on the $250 floor and the first thing it returns is that Friday.
