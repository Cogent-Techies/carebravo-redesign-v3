The structural category difference.

TangleWare™ is the structural category for disconnected, human-dependent home care platforms that require proportional staff to operate — scheduling tools your coordinator manages, billing tools your biller runs, EVV tools your compliance staff investigates, credentialing tools your HR coordinator tracks.

Every major home care software platform — including WellSky Personal Care, AlayaCare, AxisCare, Axxess, MatrixCare, and CareSmartz360 — operates on the TangleWare model. The owner becomes the integration layer between systems that do not speak to each other, and the business cannot transfer without that layer.

Work as Services is the delivery model through which CareBravo delivers nine operational functions — scheduling, EVV compliance, billing, CRM and referrals, payroll, nurse documentation, caregiver hiring, caregiver training, and project management — as completed work. Why the structural model determines exit value.

The Agency Value Scorecard tracks seven dimensions: owner independence, billing cleanliness, compliance record, caregiver retention rate, client census stability, documentation completeness, and payer diversification. What owners ask before comparing.

How is CareBravo different from home care software?

CareBravo is not home care software. It is a Work as Services delivery model where nine operational functions are delivered as completed work. Home care software platforms — WellSky, AlayaCare, AxisCare, Axxess, MatrixCare, CareSmartz360 — are TangleWare: each module surfaces work for your team to perform. CareBravo delivers the resolved scheduling, the submitted clean claim, the resolved EVV exception, without a team member operating a module to produce that output.

What is TangleWare?

TangleWare™ is the structural category for disconnected, human-dependent home care platforms that require proportional staff to operate. TangleWare is not a quality judgment — it describes the model where the owner becomes the integration layer between systems that do not speak to each other.

Can I switch from my current home care software to CareBravo?

Yes. The transition is governed by the Parallel Promise — a period of parallel operation during which existing systems run unchanged while CareBravo proves its output. Verifiable milestones must be met before any cutover. Nothing switches until milestones are proven.

Does Work as Services replace my office staff?

No. Work as Services eliminates the operational management role, not the role of caring for clients, building referral relationships, or managing your team. Office staff can redirect time spent resolving scheduling exceptions and working denied claims to client relationship management and quality oversight.
