What Autonomous Caring® is.

Autonomous Caring® is the principle that the administrative work of care — scheduling, verification, claims, documentation, compliance — belongs to a system that carries it, so the people who care can care. It is the opposite of software that flags the work and hands it back.

Up to 40% of every paid shift goes to administrative work instead of care (BMC Geriatrics, Ausserhofer et al., 2023). Everyone else sells picks and shovels — tools you operate — and calls that a service. Autonomous Caring® says the work is ours to carry.

The human stays in charge. Authority is set up front; the human signs. The nurse signs every clinical field. The owner signs what only an owner signs. The human is present and in charge — never left out of the loop, never doing the loop’s work.

What owners ask about Autonomous Caring.

What is Autonomous Caring®?

The principle that the administrative work of care belongs to a system, not to the people who care — so caregivers care, nurses supervise, and owners decide. It is the opposite of software that flags the work and hands it back.

What is an Autonomous Care OS®?

An operating system for a care agency in which the administrative work is carried by the system rather than handed back. CareBravo’s runs nine agents, one per job, orchestrated by Cary®, on one record, inside the owner’s rules, with the human signing what only a human should sign.

I have eight patients. Isn’t this a principle for big agencies?

It is the principle that decides whether you are still on the floor at eight patients or in the back office. Under about 17 active clients Autonomous lands on the $250 floor; the principle costs nothing extra.

At 90 patients, is autonomy a risk?

Autonomy without authority would be. Here authority is set up front — your rules, your thresholds, your sign-offs — and the human signs: the nurse every clinical field, you every owner decision. What you gain is a record that shows who decided what, when.

Is a person still in charge?

Yes — present, in charge, and signing. The system carries the work; the human decides. That is the whole principle.
