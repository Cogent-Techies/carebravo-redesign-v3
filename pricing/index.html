Outcome Assurance™

We guarantee that at least 99% of eligible original claims prepared and transmitted under Managed Billing go out without an error we cause.

What is guaranteed
The work we control: preparation and transmission of eligible original claims. Detectable defects are flagged before submission.
What is not
Payer adjudication. Whether Medicaid or a plan approves or pays a claim is expressly not guaranteed — no honest vendor can guarantee it.
Corrections
Included at no charge. An original claim is counted once; corrections, resubmissions, voids and replacements are never counted as additional billed revenue. Corrections never billed twice.
Eligible billed revenue
The payer-allowable value of each original claim prepared and submitted through CareBravo, counted once per claim.

“We” means CareBravo’s billing services affiliate — the entity that delivers Managed Billing, issues this guarantee, and is named in your agreement. The guarantee applies only to eligible claims prepared and submitted under Managed Billing. A “visible denial” is one accessible to us through remittance files, payer portals, claim-status responses, or information you supply. The full measurement definition is stated in your agreement.

What owners ask about pricing.

I do the schedule and the billing myself at 30 patients. Which plan?

Run the Calculator before you pick: it maps all nine jobs for your agency and recommends one of three plans against that map, never up-sold. Most owners at your size start on Autonomous — the callout, the claim check and the exceptions run inside your rules, and the evenings come back first.

I have eight patients. Isn’t $250 a lot for an agency my size?

At your size Core and Autonomous both land on the $250 floor — under about 17 active clients the price is the same — so the choice is whether you keep doing the work yourself with full visibility, or let it run inside your rules while you’re on visits. Managed Billing is for when the billing is worth handing to a person.

At 90 patients and $120K a month, what am I paying for on Managed Billing?

Accountability with a number attached: 4.47% of eligible billed revenue buys preparation and submission of every eligible claim by our billing specialists, the correction cycle counted once, weekly dated reporting by payer, and a written 99% guarantee on the work we control. Your biller keeps the payer relationships and the judgment calls.

What does “eligible billed revenue” mean?

The payer-allowable value of each original claim prepared and submitted through CareBravo, counted once per claim. Corrections, resubmissions, voids and replacements are never counted again. It is not what you collect; it is what you billed, once.

What does the 99% guarantee actually cover?

That at least 99% of eligible original claims are prepared and transmitted without an error we cause, on Managed Billing. Payer adjudication is expressly not guaranteed. Corrections are included at no charge and never billed twice.

Is there a contract, an implementation fee, or per-user pricing?

No implementation fee, no seats, no per-user fees. The floor is $250 a month on every plan. Thirty days’ notice either way.

Is CareBravo software or a service?

More than software. The platform is where the work lives on every plan; the plan decides who carries the work — your team on Core, the nine agents on Autonomous, and our billing specialists behind the written 99% guarantee on Managed Billing.
