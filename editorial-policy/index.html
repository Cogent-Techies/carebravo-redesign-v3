Editorial policy

How this site is written, sourced and reviewed.

Every number carries its source. Every page carries its review date. Nobody calls unless you ask.

Sources.

Government sources first, named: CMS, HHS OIG, DOJ, KFF, MACPAC and state agencies.

Industry surveys are cited by description — “the industry’s largest annual home-care benchmarking survey, n≈1,000, 2025”; “a 2025 survey of 145 agency leaders” — because a vendor’s name as the publisher of a statistic is still a vendor’s name on the page. Peer-reviewed research is cited by journal and author.

A number without a source does not appear. A number that is our own observation says so. A number that is our own customer data states its year and its n. Dollar figures are paired to the agency size they were calculated for and never crossed.

What we refuse

Feature counts. Competitor names and scoreboards. Testimonials without written consent. Generated faces standing in for real people. Growth percentages. Any promise of payer adjudication.

Imagery

Stills on this site are generated, art-directed to one canon, and depict no real customer, caregiver or client. Captions say what the image shows. No stock photography.

Review

Every page shows a review date. Pages are reviewed by the founder, Anand Chaturvedi, and re-reviewed when a source, a rate, a rule or a plan changes. Georgia specifics are verified against current DCH guidance before publication and re-verified quarterly. The installed-base figure is refreshed quarterly with its date.

Corrections

If a number on this site is wrong, tell us and it changes, with the correction dated on the page. Write to the address in your agreement, or reply to any email from Team CareBravo.
