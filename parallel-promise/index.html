What Parallel Promise™ is.

The Parallel Promise™ is CareBravo’s transition standard: two weeks in which the agency’s existing systems and CareBravo run in parallel on real data, with nothing cut over until every job verifies — and the parallel run extended, not the standard lowered, if it does not.

You have switched platforms before. You know the cost when it goes wrong: the lost data, the retraining, the dual-payroll month. The Parallel Promise™ exists because that month should never happen.

The first 90 days: weeks one and two in parallel. Weeks three to six in full transition, with your previous systems available as backup. Weeks seven to twelve on CareBravo alone, with the deltas measured across all nine jobs.

What owners ask about Parallel Promise.

What is the Parallel Promise™?

Two weeks in which your current systems and CareBravo run side by side on your real data, caregivers and clients, with nothing cut over until every job verifies. If anything isn’t clean at the end of week two, the run extends.

I have eight patients. Do I need two weeks?

Small agencies have the least room for a bad cutover, so yes — and at your size the parallel run is short work: the same visits verified in both places, the same claims built twice, compared. It costs you nothing extra.

I run 90 patients on three systems. What does the parallel run actually compare?

Every job, by the numbers: hours verified, claims built, exceptions found, renewals due, referrals answered — in your systems and in CareBravo, side by side, weekly and dated. Cutover happens job by job as each verifies, with your previous systems available as backup through week six.

What if it isn’t clean at week two?

The run extends. The standard is verification, not a date. You will read that in the weekly report before anyone asks you to depend on anything.

Is the Parallel Promise™ a guarantee?

It is a standard we work to. The word guarantee on this site attaches to one thing only: the written 99% on Managed Billing, titled Outcome Assurance™.
