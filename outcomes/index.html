What owners ask about the nine.

What does CareBravo actually do for a home care agency?

It does the nine jobs a Medicaid home care agency has to do — scheduling and payroll, compliance, billing, communication, documentation, referrals and reputation, hiring, retention and training — on one record, inside the owner's rules, and reports on them weekly, dated, including what went wrong on our side. The owner signs what only an owner can sign; the nurse signs every clinical field.

Why nine, and not a feature list?

Because a Medicaid agency has nine jobs, not ninety features. Every task an owner does at night belongs to one of the nine, and the CareDrain™ Calculator scores all nine for your agency so the work leaves your desk in the order it costs you most.

Do I have to hand over all nine at once?

No. All nine run on the same record from day one on every plan; which ones are carried for you depends on the plan. The Calculator recommends one of three plans against your map — never up-sold.

Is CareBravo software or a service?

More than software. The platform is where the work lives; the plan decides who carries it — your team on Core, the nine agents on Autonomous, and our billing specialists with the written 99% guarantee on Managed Billing.

Which job would you hand over first?

The Calculator maps your CareDrain™ across all nine and recommends one plan against your map — never sold up. Your agency’s name on the answer.
