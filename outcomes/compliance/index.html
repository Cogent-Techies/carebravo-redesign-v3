What owners ask about compliance.

How do I get my EVV exception rate down when I don’t even know what it is?

Start by seeing it daily instead of discovering it in a remittance file. An exception is a denial with a three-week head start; worked the day it occurs — the dead phone, the address that would not resolve, the missed clock-out — most never reach a claim. CareBravo shows the rate weekly, dated, and works the exceptions inside your rules so the number you see is the number that is left.

I have eight patients and no compliance person. Is it too early for this?

It is exactly when it matters: a first-year survey finding is an existential problem at eight patients and a correction at eighty. Credentials tracked before they lapse, EVV worked daily, and every note on one record is what a surveyor asks for. Under about 17 active clients, CareBravo Core and CareBravo Autonomous both land on the $250 monthly floor, so the price is the same at your size and the question is only whether you keep doing this yourself with full visibility, or let it run inside your rules while you’re on visits.

I have a compliance coordinator. What changes at 90 patients?

She stops being the person who finds things. Open exceptions by age, credential lapses caught by system rather than by audit, policy currency, survey response time — reported weekly, dated. What you gain is an auditable trail a plan network or a buyer can read in an afternoon, and a coordinator who spends her time on judgment rather than discovery.

How do I prepare for a Georgia DCH survey?

You prepare by not preparing: the record that is complete every day is the survey packet. Visits verified, credentials current, notes signed by the nurse, policies dated — all on one record, so the response to a notice is what already exists rather than fourteen days of assembly.

Is this compliance software, or do you handle compliance?

More than compliance software. The platform is the record on every plan. On Core your team keeps it current with full visibility; on Autonomous exceptions, credentials and bulletins are worked inside your rules and reported weekly, dated.
