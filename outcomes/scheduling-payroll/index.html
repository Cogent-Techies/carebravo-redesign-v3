What owners ask about scheduling & payroll.

Why do I still end up covering callouts myself at 30 patients?

Because coverage is a matching problem the schedule was never built to solve: who is cleared for this client, close enough, under their hours, and awake. A scheduler solves it by phone at 5 AM; you solve it by taking the shift. CareBravo matches the callout against every cleared caregiver the moment it arrives and confirms the cover, so what reaches you is the one shift nobody can take — and that one, you decide.

I have eight patients and four caregivers. Is scheduling worth paying for at my size?

At four caregivers the callout is usually you, so the value isn’t the board — it’s the hours. Verified once, paid right, and billed from the same record, so Friday night stops being payroll night. Under about 17 active clients, CareBravo Core and CareBravo Autonomous both land on the $250 monthly floor, so the price is the same at your size and the question is only whether you keep doing this yourself with full visibility, or let it run inside your rules while you’re on visits.

I have a full-time scheduler. What changes at 90 patients?

Your scheduler stops being the exception path. Open-shift rate, travel time as a share of paid time, payroll error rate — reported weekly, dated, so you can state them. Coverage runs on rules you set; your scheduler handles the calls that need judgment. What you gain is a schedule that is documented and auditable, which is what a plan network or a buyer asks to see.

Does CareBravo handle EVV clock-in and payroll for Georgia waivers?

Yes. EVV-compliant clock-in and clock-out, exceptions worked the day they occur, hours reconciled to the visit, and payroll calculated by waiver and state rate — including travel time and overtime — from the same record the claim is built on.

Is this scheduling software, or do you do the scheduling?

More than scheduling software. The platform is where the schedule lives on every plan. On Core your scheduler works it with full visibility; on Autonomous it fills itself inside your rules; Managed Scheduling, added when you ask, puts CareBravo affiliate schedulers on the field work.
