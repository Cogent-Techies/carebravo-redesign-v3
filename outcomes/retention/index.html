What owners ask about retention.

Why do 80% of my new hires leave in the first 100 days?

Rarely for the wage, which you don’t set — for the schedule that changed without asking, the check that was short, the notes that took an hour. Those are the levers you control. CareBravo verifies hours once and pays them right, offers schedule changes rather than imposing them, drafts the notes on the record, and puts the 30-, 60- and 90-day check-ins on the calendar so the cliff is watched, not discovered.

I have eight patients and four caregivers. Losing one is losing a quarter of my agency. What helps at my size?

The same three things, and they cost nothing extra: her check right every time, her schedule respected, her paperwork light. At four caregivers you can have every check-in conversation yourself — the record just makes sure they happen. Under about 17 active clients, CareBravo Core and CareBravo Autonomous both land on the $250 monthly floor, so the price is the same at your size and the question is only whether you keep doing this yourself with full visibility, or let it run inside your rules while you’re on visits.

I have 30 caregivers and an HR coordinator. What changes at 90 patients?

Turnover stops being a feeling and becomes a number: 100-day retention, voluntary turnover, exit reasons by category — reported weekly, dated. Your coordinator spends her time on the conversations; the pay accuracy, schedule fairness and check-in cadence run inside your rules. What you gain is a workforce number a plan network or a buyer trusts.

Should I just pay more?

Medicaid rates set a ceiling you didn’t choose, so no — this page will never tell you to pay a wage you can’t. Schedule respect and pay accuracy outrank the wage rate as predictors of staying in the first 100 days, and those two you control.

Is this retention software, or do you do the retention?

More than retention software. The platform keeps the first 100 days on the record on every plan. On Core your team runs the check-ins with full visibility; on Autonomous check-ins, schedule fairness and pay accuracy run inside your rules — and you have the conversations.
