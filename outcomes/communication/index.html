What owners ask about communication.

How do I stop caregivers from texting my personal phone at all hours?

Give them a line that answers. Caregivers text the owner because the owner is the only number that resolves anything; they stop the week another line resolves it faster. CareBravo routes caregiver questions, callouts and family updates to a line that answers inside your rules, documents every exchange on the client record, and escalates to you only what needs an owner.

I have eight patients. My cell is the agency. Is this for me?

Especially. At eight patients every family has your number and every caregiver uses it; the first thing this gives back is your evenings. Families are told about coverage changes before they ask, and the caregiver line answers before you would have. Under about 17 active clients, CareBravo Core and CareBravo Autonomous both land on the $250 monthly floor, so the price is the same at your size and the question is only whether you keep doing this yourself with full visibility, or let it run inside your rules while you’re on visits.

I have an office line and a family portal. What changes at 90 patients?

Response time becomes a number you can state: case managers answered inside four hours, family complaints as a share of clients, after-hours escalations reaching you per week. Communication becomes documented and transferable — which is what an MCO relationship, and eventually a buyer, is really evaluating.

Do case managers and discharge planners get a faster answer?

Yes, and that is the revenue half of this. The agency that answers a referral in two hours wins the census; the one that takes three days trains its referral sources to call someone else. Case manager calls and referral requests are answered inside four business hours, with the record attached.

Is this messaging software, or do you handle the communication?

More than messaging software. The platform keeps one thread per client on the record on every plan. On Core your office answers it with full visibility; on Autonomous families, caregivers and case managers are answered inside your rules, and only escalations reach you.
