What owners ask about training.

How do I deliver mandated training without losing shift coverage?

Stop delivering it as an event. Modules of ten to fifteen minutes fit the gaps between visits; delivered on her phone, in her language, and logged on the record, the same hours that used to cost a shift cost nothing. CareBravo assigns training by waiver, role and language, delivers it in the workflow, verifies competency, and schedules renewals before anything lapses.

I have eight patients and I train my four caregivers myself. Is this worth it at my size?

It is worth it the day a surveyor asks for training records and you have attendance sheets. Assignments, completion, competency and dates on one record is what she wants to see — and it takes you out of the classroom. Under about 17 active clients, CareBravo Core and CareBravo Autonomous both land on the $250 monthly floor, so the price is the same at your size and the question is only whether you keep doing this yourself with full visibility, or let it run inside your rules while you’re on visits.

I have a training coordinator and a Spanish-speaking workforce. What changes at 90 patients?

Completion rate on the mandated cadence, hours per caregiver, language coverage, lapses caught by system rather than by audit — reported weekly, dated. Your coordinator designs; delivery, verification and renewal run inside your rules. What you gain is a training function that is transferable and audit-defensible.

Does this cover SOURCE, NOW and COMP training requirements in Georgia?

Yes. Waiver-specific training is a documented audit point in SOURCE, NOW and COMP reviews, and it is assigned by waiver here, so the caregiver on a SOURCE client has completed SOURCE training before her first shift with that client — logged, dated, signed.

Is this an LMS, or do you do the training?

More than an LMS. The platform keeps the training record on every plan. On Core your team delivers and logs with full visibility; on Autonomous assignments, delivery and renewals run inside your rules, and competency is verified and logged.
