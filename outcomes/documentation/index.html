What owners ask about documentation.

Why do I keep losing referrals to agencies that aren’t better than mine?

Because the planner’s test is speed, not quality: the agency that answers in two hours with a start date wins the census. CareBravo triages the referral, runs eligibility, chases the authorization and matches a caregiver the day it arrives, so your answer is a date, not a promise to call back.

I have eight patients and I do intake, assessments and notes myself. Where does this fit?

At your size the first thing it gives back is the referral you’d have lost to a slow week — and the second is the notes you write at night. The record drafts the assessment and the visit note; you review and sign. Under about 17 active clients, CareBravo Core and CareBravo Autonomous both land on the $250 monthly floor, so the price is the same at your size and the question is only whether you keep doing this yourself with full visibility, or let it run inside your rules while you’re on visits.

I have an RN supervisor and an intake coordinator. What changes at 90 patients?

Your RN stops spending most of her paid hours writing and starts spending them supervising. Referral-to-first-visit days, conversion rate, reassessments past due, authorization days pending — reported weekly, dated. What you gain is a chain of evidence a surveyor or a buyer can follow from referral to claim without a gap.

Does the nurse still sign the documentation?

Always. The record drafts; the nurse reviews and signs every clinical field — assessments, care plans, supervisory visits, incident reports. Authority is set up front, and a note the nurse has not signed is not a note.

Is this an EMR, or do you handle the documentation?

More than an EMR. The platform is the one chain from referral to reassessment on every plan. On Core your team writes on it with full visibility; on Autonomous referrals are answered, records drafted and cadence kept inside your rules — and the nurse signs.
