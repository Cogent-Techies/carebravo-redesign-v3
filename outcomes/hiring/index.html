What owners ask about hiring.

Why does it take me three weeks to hire a CNA when she’s ready on Monday?

Because the steps run in series and each waits for you: see the application, schedule the interview, start the check, make the offer, start onboarding. Run in parallel, the same steps fit inside a week. CareBravo screens the day she applies, starts the background check before the interview, and readies onboarding, so the offer — yours — is the only step that waits.

I have eight patients and hire one caregiver every few months. Does this apply to me?

It applies most at the moment you turn down a referral because there is no one to staff it. A pipeline that is always warm — screened applicants, checks started — means the next referral is a yes. Under about 17 active clients, CareBravo Core and CareBravo Autonomous both land on the $250 monthly floor, so the price is the same at your size and the question is only whether you keep doing this yourself with full visibility, or let it run inside your rules while you’re on visits.

I have a recruiter. What changes at 90 patients?

Cost per hire, time to first shift and pipeline conversion become numbers you can state, weekly and dated. Your recruiter spends her time on the interview and the offer; the screening, checks and onboarding run inside your rules. What you gain is operating leverage on hiring spend — the same recruiter, twice the throughput.

How long does a GCHEX background check take, and can you shorten it?

The state sets the clock; nobody can shorten the state. What can be shortened is when it starts — the day she applies rather than after the interview — so the check runs while everything else does, and the first shift waits on nothing.

Is this recruiting software, or do you do the hiring?

More than recruiting software. The platform keeps one pipeline on the record on every plan. On Core your recruiter works it with full visibility; on Autonomous applicants are screened, checks started and onboarding run inside your rules — and you make the offer.
