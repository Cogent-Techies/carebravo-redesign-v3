My referrals dropped and I can’t tell why. Where do I start?

Count them by source for the last twelve months — most owners find three of four come from one person. Then ask what that person’s successor, or a network manager, could read about your agency in ninety seconds. CareBravo tracks referrals by source weekly so a drop is named the week it starts, and keeps the record — outcomes, staffing, coverage — that lets a stranger check you and choose you.

I have eight patients. How do I get my first referrals?

By being the agency that answers a case manager in two hours with a start date, and by having one page she can read about you. At your stage the record is small, so it’s easy to keep legible from the start. Under about 17 active clients, CareBravo Core and CareBravo Autonomous both land on the $250 monthly floor, so the price is the same at your size and the question is only whether you keep doing this yourself with full visibility, or let it run inside your rules while you’re on visits.

I’ve run this agency for nineteen years. What changes when a plan narrows its network?

The plan keeps the agencies it can evaluate. Nineteen years of reputation is invisible to a network manager running adequacy math unless it is written down: outcomes, staffing, coverage, quality, dated. CareBravo keeps that record current from your operations so the letter is answered from what exists. And the agency a plan cannot evaluate is the agency a buyer cannot price — same record, both doors.
