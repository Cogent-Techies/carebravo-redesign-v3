What owners ask about getting started.

How long before the evenings come back?

The first two weeks run in parallel, so you feel it in week three: the callout matched before you dial, the claims out clean, the exception worked the day it happens. By week seven the previous systems are off and the weekly report is the only thing you read on a Friday.

I have eight patients. Is the parallel run overkill for me?

It is two weeks of insurance you don’t pay for. Small agencies have the least room for a bad cutover; running both ways on your real data is how you never have one.

I have staff who run three systems today. What happens to them?

They keep their jobs and lose the reconciliation. The record replaces the three systems; the routine runs inside your rules; your people spend their day on the calls and decisions that need a person. At 90 patients what you gain is a set of numbers you can state, weekly and dated.

What is the Parallel Promise?

For two weeks your current workflow and CareBravo run side by side, on your real data, caregivers and clients. Nothing moves until verification is complete; if anything is not clean at the end of week two, the parallel run extends. The transition is on a verification standard, not a timeline.
