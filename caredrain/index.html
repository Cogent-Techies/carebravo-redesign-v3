What CareDrain™ is.

CareDrain™ is the daily loss a Medicaid home care agency suffers to administrative work — the screen time, the reconciliation, the corrections, the coverage — across five vectors: Economic, Talent, Time, Stability and Energy.

The Economic drain is the claim that denied and the hour that wasn’t billed. The Talent drain is the caregiver who left in her first 100 days because her check was short. The Time drain is the 8 hours a week per care worker that go to screens (BMC Geriatrics, Ausserhofer et al., 2023). The Stability drain is the survey you can’t prepare for in fourteen days. The Energy drain is the owner at the kitchen table at 9:40 PM.

Census is a KPI, not a drain. The five are the five.
